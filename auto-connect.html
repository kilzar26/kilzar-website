03 / Deliberately lightweight

Add a front door.
Don't replace your whole shop stack.

Auto Connect is not positioned as a full shop-management system. It is designed to solve a narrower problem cleanly: capture customer requests and make callbacks easier to manage.

04 / Pilot boundaries

Clear about what it
does—and doesn't—do.

It does

Collect customer and vehicle information, organize concerns and timing, and track staff follow-up status.

It does not

Diagnose the vehicle, guarantee appointments or customer traffic, replace the shop's service-management system, or make repair decisions.

Shop responsibility

The participating shop remains responsible for customer communication, estimates, service, repair decisions, and its own operational policies. Auto Connect does not automatically diagnose vehicles or send customer SMS/email messages in the current pilot.
