Full description:
KRAI Casebook helps you keep a clear record of a vehicle problem from the first observation through the repair and follow-up check.

Create a case, write down symptoms and evidence, record the work performed, and check the result after the repair. Review your repair history when the same issue returns or you need to share a timeline.

Your cases are stored locally in the app's browser storage on your device. Export a JSON backup and import it when needed. Clearing app or browser data can erase cases that you have not backed up.

KRAI Casebook is a manual record tool. It does not scan a vehicle, connect to an OBD device, diagnose faults automatically, or replace a qualified mechanic. There is no paid plan in this release.
