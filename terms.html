Pilot limitations

Casebook records and compares information you enter. It does not connect to a vehicle, verify that an observation is correct, diagnose faults, establish that a repair succeeded, or assess whether a vehicle is safe to drive. Use qualified inspection and appropriate service information for vehicle decisions. Save your own backup; local browser records may be lost if browser storage is cleared or the device changes.
