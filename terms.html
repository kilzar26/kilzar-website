KRAI Casebook

Kilzar Technologies LLC permits visitors to use the free KRAI Casebook pilot for their own vehicle records. Casebook records and compares information you enter. It does not connect to a vehicle, verify that an observation is correct, diagnose faults, establish that a repair succeeded, or assess whether a vehicle is safe to drive.

Use qualified inspection and appropriate service information for vehicle decisions. Save your own backup; local browser records may be lost if browser storage is cleared or the device changes.

Kilzar Auto Connect

Auto Connect is a customer-intake and callback workflow for participating repair shops. Submission of a request does not guarantee an appointment, diagnosis, repair outcome, response time, or availability. Participating shops remain responsible for their own customer communication, estimates, services, and repair decisions.
