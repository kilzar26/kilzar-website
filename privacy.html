Privacy

KRAI Casebook pilot

Vehicle cases you create in the pilot are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. Kilzar does not receive those cases through the pilot app. You can export a JSON backup, and you control where that file is kept or shared. Clearing browser data or using another device can remove access to locally saved cases.

If you submit the pilot request form, it sends your email address, selected role, description of your need, and consent choice to Kilzar through the website's hosting provider. Kilzar uses this information to review the request and contact you about the pilot. Do not include VINs, addresses, or sensitive vehicle records in that form. You may request deletion or opt out of pilot emails by contacting info@kilzartech.com.

Website and contact

Information voluntarily emailed to Kilzar is used to respond and for related business communication. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate and secure the website. Casebook does not use advertising trackers or send saved case contents to an AI service.
