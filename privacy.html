Privacy

Plain-language privacy information for the public website and current pilots.

KRAI Casebook pilot

Vehicle cases you create in Casebook are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. Kilzar does not receive those saved vehicle cases through the pilot app. You can export a JSON backup, and you control where that file is kept or shared. Clearing browser data or using another device can remove access to locally saved cases.

If you submit the Casebook pilot form, the information you choose to provide—such as email address, role, description of your need, and contact consent—is sent to Kilzar through Netlify Forms. That information is used to review pilot interest and respond to you. The form does not send your locally saved vehicle cases.

Kilzar Auto Connect pilot

Auto Connect is a repair-shop intake and callback workflow. Information entered into that pilot may include customer contact details, vehicle details, the reported concern, preferred timing, and the shop's follow-up status. That information is used to help the participating repair shop review and manage the request. Do not submit information you do not want the receiving shop to use for that purpose.

Website and business contact

Information voluntarily emailed to Kilzar is used to respond and for related business communication. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate and secure the website. Casebook does not use advertising trackers or send saved case contents to an AI service.

To request deletion of pilot-contact information or opt out of Kilzar communications, email info@kilzartech.com.
